Trilyt, for riders and travellers

The group stays on one route, whoever is leading.

Trilyt plans the ride, navigates it and keeps everyone in sync on the road, so a group of five behaves like one.

  • Turn-by-turn navigation for two-wheelersRoutes chosen for bikes, not cars, with clear guidance on every turn.
  • Ride syncSee every rider live on the same map, so nobody gets lost at a fork.
  • Hey Trilyt voice messagesRiders on Bluetooth speak to the group hands-free. Trilyt records and sends the message.
  • Trip plans and budgetsDay-by-day plans with progress on the road, and a trip budget split per person across stays, food, fuel and tolls.
  • Riding communitiesFind riding clubs near you, request to join, or start your own.
